🌍 Disaster Categories

Learn about different types of disasters.

🌊

Flood

Move to higher ground and avoid walking or driving through flood water.

🌪️

Cyclone

Stay indoors and follow official cyclone warnings and evacuation orders.

🌎

Earthquake

Drop, cover and hold during an earthquake.

🔥

Fire

Leave the building safely and never use elevators during a fire.

⛰️

Landslide

Move away from unstable slopes and affected areas.

🌊

Tsunami

Move quickly to higher ground after a tsunami warning.

🌡️

Heatwave

Stay hydrated and avoid direct sunlight during extreme heat.

🏢

Building Collapse

Stay away from damaged structures and contact rescue services.

🛡️ Safety Guidelines

Follow these basic precautions.

🟢 Before a Disaster

  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Keep important documents safe.
  • Save emergency contact numbers.
  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Know your nearest safe location.
  • Store drinking water and food.

🔴 During a Disaster

  • Stay calm.
  • Follow official instructions.
  • Move to a safe location.
  • Avoid damaged buildings.
  • Help children and elderly people.
  • Do not spread unverified information.

🔵 After a Disaster

  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings.
  • Report dangerous conditions.
  • Follow government instructions.
  • Use emergency resources responsibly.

🎒 Emergency Kit

  • Drinking water
  • First-aid kit
  • Flashlight
  • Power bank
  • Important documents
  • Basic food supplies
